# SEO / AEO Query-to-Page Matrix

Status: planning artifact
Updated: 2026-08-20
Branch: `agent/architecture-sandbox`

## Purpose

Define the first evidence-backed Spanish page opportunities for Barcelona before activating new routes.

This matrix is not a keyword-volume forecast. No search-volume numbers are assumed. Priority is based on:

1. commercial value to Vital Vibe Construction;
2. observed search-result intent and current Barcelona competitor patterns;
3. strength of first-party evidence already available in the Knowledge Repository, project media, or article sandbox;
4. usefulness as a direct answer source for search and AI systems;
5. ability to build a distinct page without generic or invented copy.

## Non-negotiable planning rules

- Do not activate a route only because a keyword exists.
- Every public page must have page-owned content and a distinct reason to exist.
- Prefer first-party project evidence over generic market claims.
- Market price ranges from external sites are research context only; do not publish them as VVC facts.
- VVC pricing claims must come from confirmed internal records and include visible limitations.
- Do not create neighborhood or city landing-page farms by swapping place names.
- Do not publish guarantees, certifications, licenses, response times, staffing claims, financing, permit-management promises, or material-brand claims unless verified in the source of truth.
- Keep Spanish coverage strong before introducing crawlable EN/RU variants.
- Proposed guide routes remain inactive until a guide/article route family is defined in architecture contracts.

## Priority definitions

- `P0`: launch/discovery-critical or strongest immediate commercial opportunity.
- `P1`: high-value next expansion with strong evidence.
- `P2`: useful supporting content that should follow the core commercial cluster.
- `READY`: current evidence is sufficient for drafting/modeling without inventing facts.
- `NEEDS-CONTENT`: entity exists, but page-owned content must be written and verified.
- `NEEDS-FACTS`: important claims or commercial details are not yet confirmed.
- `EXISTING`: route already exists; work is optimization/internal-linking rather than route creation.

## Recommended execution order

### Wave 0 — discovery gate

1. Confirm how production will serve `site-next` as the public document root.
2. Deploy the current eight-page projection without changing canonical URLs.
3. Verify HTTP 200, assets, canonical tags, robots, sitemap, structured data, mobile rendering and Search Console discovery.

No content expansion can create meaningful organic discovery until this gate is complete.

### Wave 1 — highest commercial return

Build or strengthen these pages first:

1. existing `reformas integrales` service;
2. Spanish renovation-price guide;
3. kitchen service;
4. bathroom service;
5. electrical service.

Reason: these combine strong commercial intent with either existing evidence or an obvious first-party evidence path. Current Barcelona SERPs heavily emphasize price, scope, duration and technical risk. VVC should compete with better evidence rather than broader unsupported claims.

### Wave 2 — evidence moat

Build:

1. old-building renovation guide;
2. uneven-floor / leveling guide;
3. electrical-renovation guide;
4. painting service;
5. painting price/preparation guide;
6. masonry service.

These pages turn VVC project evidence into answerable technical content that generic landing pages cannot reproduce safely.

### Wave 3 — supporting decision content

Build:

1. kitchen planning guide;
2. bathroom waterproofing guide;
3. how to review a renovation estimate.

These are valuable for AEO and conversion, but should follow the core commercial cluster and must not introduce unverified promises.

## Intentionally deferred opportunities

Do not prioritize these until evidence exists:

- one page per Barcelona neighborhood with repeated service copy;
- `mejor empresa de reformas Barcelona` self-ranking pages;
- guarantee/warranty pages without a confirmed business policy;
- financing pages without a confirmed financing offer;
- permit/license-management claims without confirmed operational scope;
- structural-safety advice that requires professional verification;
- exact material-brand comparison pages based only on competitor content;
- project image-detail pages;
- EN/RU mirrors before Spanish topical coverage is stronger.

## External search-result observations used for planning

Research sampled on 2026-08-20. Current Barcelona search results repeatedly emphasize:

- price per square metre and example apartment sizes for integral renovation;
- complete versus partial bathroom and kitchen renovation;
- duration and scope;
- old-building conditions and hidden installations;
- waterproofing, ventilation and drainage for bathrooms;
- wall preparation as a major factor in painting cost.

These observations establish user-intent patterns only. Competitor facts, prices, guarantees and claims are not VVC source material.

Representative research sources included current pages from ReformaTuCasa, ERA Architects, ObraDirecta, Cronoshare, Habitissimo and other Barcelona renovation providers/marketplaces. Before publication, VVC pages must rely on the Knowledge Repository and separately verified first-party facts rather than reproducing competitor data.

## Definition of done for this planning stage

The matrix is complete when:

- 10–15 candidate pages are ranked;
- each candidate has a proposed route and intent;
- evidence readiness and missing facts are explicit;
- no new route has been activated merely because it appears in the matrix;
- the next implementation wave is unambiguous;
- the repository remains green and generated projections remain unchanged.
