What a complete renovation includes

A complete renovation brings space preparation, building work, installations and finishes together within one coordinated scope. Each project is reviewed to define the work required before a quote is prepared.

Complete renovation coordination, from preparation and installations to final finishes.

Building work

Electrical work

Kitchens

Bathrooms

Painting

Surface preparation and painting finishes for homes and commercial spaces.

Process

How we work

The process adapts to the actual scope of each renovation; we do not assume work items or timelines before reviewing the project.

  1. 01

    Initial contact

    We gather the available information about the space, your needs and the result you want to achieve.

  2. 02

    Project review

    We review the condition of the space and the information needed to understand the work the renovation may require.

  3. 03

    Scope definition

    We define the building work, installations, kitchen, bathrooms, painting and finishes included in the project.

  4. 04

    Quote

    We prepare a proposal based on the agreed scope and review it before organising the work.

  5. 05

    Work coordination

    We organise the different parts of the renovation so that execution follows the agreed scope.

  6. 06

    Final review

    At the end of the work, we review what has been completed and any remaining points, if there are any.

Frequently asked questions

Before planning your renovation

Answers based on the scope and services currently documented in our records.

What does a complete renovation include?

The scope may include preparation and building work, electrical installations, kitchen, bathrooms, painting and finishes. The exact work is defined after reviewing each project.

How is the quote prepared?

The quote is prepared from the condition of the space, the stated needs and the defined scope of the different renovation work.

Can only part of a home be renovated?

Yes. We also carry out specific work such as kitchens, bathrooms, electrical work, painting or building work when a project does not require a complete renovation.

Who coordinates the different trades?

For a complete renovation, we coordinate the work included in the scope to organise preparation, installations and finishes.

Can I use the electrical planner before requesting a quote?

Yes. The beta electrical planner lets you organise an initial proposal for equipment, electrical points and lighting circuits before requesting a quote.

Do you work in Barcelona and surrounding areas?

Yes. Vital Vibe Construction serves Barcelona and surrounding areas.
