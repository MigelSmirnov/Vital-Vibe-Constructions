Wall painting: the price starts with preparation

A wall may look ready for paint until a crack reveals a loose coating. The estimate depends on the substrate and preparation, as well as applying paint.

01€4–6/m²: what this price covers

Our indicative €4–6/m² rate covers labour only on already prepared walls, painted in a single colour. Materials cost extra. Crack repairs, removal of loose coatings and other preparation work are priced separately.

For this scope, application includes primer and two coats of paint, with drying time between coats. The estimate specifies which surfaces will be painted and what protection the space needs.

02A small crack can change the scope

The first photograph shows a fine crack. The second shows coating coming away and exposing the substrate. These images document the condition found; they do not establish the cause of the crack on their own.

We review the substrate before finishing. Where coating is loose, the estimate needs to include removal and repair before painting. Covering the visible line does not necessarily describe all the work required.

03An occupied home needs protection

Floors, doors, windows, furniture, kitchen units, sockets and switches need protection appropriate to the work. A furnished home requires different preparation from an empty room within a full renovation.

The condition of the existing paint also matters: loose areas and outstanding repairs are identified before the finish is specified.

04Spray gun or roller

We use spray equipment where it suits the space and the work. Spraying requires protecting surfaces that must not receive paint. In a furnished home or around numerous details, a roller may be more convenient.

We choose the tool after inspecting the property. An estimate should specify preparation, primer, coat count, materials and protection, so comparisons are not based on a square-metre figure alone.

05What to compare in estimates

  • Wall condition and included repairs.
  • Protection of furniture and other surfaces.
  • Primer and number of coats.
  • Number of colours and application method.
  • Whether materials are included or charged separately.
  • The measured surface and final cleaning scope.

The final price is confirmed after inspecting the space. The €4–6/m² reference is not a rate for repairing and repainting any wall.
